How the verdict is calculated

The Shame Index is a weighted score from 0 to 100. These six overlapping signals emphasize repeated poor scoring; the weights are a league debate starter, not an objective measure of skill.

All recorded finalized scores count throughout the season, including playoffs and consolation games. Guillotine results do not remove anyone from this ranking. Each week is compared with its recorded scoring field. Teams absent from a playoff week receive no result for that week, and limited coverage is shown above. Conflicting or missing scores cause that week to be skipped.

The 2018–2025 seasons come from the supplied ESPN export; other seasons use ESPN’s public API. Historical manager names are matched after normalizing case, spacing, and duplicate names. Current ESPN member names join to that history when the complete owner group matches. Unresolved identities stay separate; team-name changes alone never link managers.

Each scored week carries equal weight. Fewer than five weeks is marked “Small sample.” Average points are shown for context; the index compares results within each week. Live projections and unavailable injury or lineup-efficiency data are not used.
